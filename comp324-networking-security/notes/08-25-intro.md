### Formulas for quantifying risk
```
Single Loss Expectancy (SLE) = 
Asset Value (AV) * Exposure Factor (EF)
```
```
Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE) =
Single Loss Expectancy (SLE) * Annual Rate of Occurrence (ARO)
```

### Once Identified - Four Choices
- **Acceptance** - Find the cost of losing is worth the risk
- **Avoidance** - taking action so no risk chance
- **Transference** - transfer responsibility to another company. Outsourcing
- **Mitigation** - take steps to reduce threat or likelihood of happening

## Types of Threats
### Malware - Malicious Software
- viruses
- trojan horses
- spyware (cookies key loggers)
- logic bombs
  - lay dormant until some logical condition is met, often a specific date

### Viruses
- One of the two most common types of malware
- designed to replicate and spread
- usually spreads through email
- uses system resources, causing network slowdowns or stoppage
